Delete Your Account

How to request deletion of your Nyaya account and associated personal data.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Open the Nyaya app and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Ask Nyaya.
  3. Send a message asking us to delete your account. Use the same email you signed in with.

We will delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account that is no longer required.

Important

Account deletion is permanent. Saved topics on the device are removed when you uninstall the app. Deleting your Nyaya account does not cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Cancel that in your store account to stop future charges.

What may be retained

Some records may be kept when required by law, to prevent fraud, or to complete a subscription already billed by the store. Those records are kept only as long as needed.
