Privacy Policy

How Nyaya collects, uses, and protects information in this learning app.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

1. Who we are

Nyaya is an educational civic guide for India. It helps you understand which office to approach and what usually happens next. It is not a lawyer, not legal advice, and not a government service.

2. What we store

If you create an account: name, email, language, and the Indian State or Union Territory you pick so we can show matching office names. Saved topics stay on the phone. Optional questions you send through Ask Nyaya include the text you type. Catalog content is downloaded as one public file.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not show ads. We do not use the catalog to profile you. We do not store card numbers.

4. Payments and third parties

Subscription payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store. RevenueCat tells the app whether your plan is active. Sign-in and the published catalog use Google Firebase. Standard Firebase logs may exist for security. Email verification is sent by Firebase Authentication.

5. Your choices

You may change language and State anytime. You may sign out. To request deletion of your account, use Ask Nyaya in the app or follow the account deletion page.

6. Children

Nyaya is written for adults learning civic paths. It is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when we publish a new catalog version. The date on this page will change when we do.
