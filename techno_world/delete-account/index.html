Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Technology World account and associated personal data directly from the app.

Last updated: August 12, 2026

How to Delete Your Technology World Account

Technology World provides an in-app account deletion option. You do not need to contact us by email to request deletion.

  1. Open the Technology World app.
  2. Open your Profile or Settings section.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the information shown about account deletion.
  5. Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.
Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once the deletion process has been completed, your account and eligible associated data cannot be restored.

What Data Is Deleted?

When you delete your Technology World account, we delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account that is no longer required for legitimate or legal purposes.

This may include:

  • Your account and profile information.
  • Your email address associated with the account.
  • Learning progress and account-specific learning data.
  • User preferences associated with your account.
  • Other personal information directly associated with your account.

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained when necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, or satisfy legitimate business requirements.

Where retention is required, the information will be retained only for the period reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and will be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Deleting your Technology World account does not necessarily cancel a subscription purchased through Google Play.

If you have an active subscription, please manage or cancel the subscription through your Google Play account to prevent future subscription charges.

Purchase and transaction records may be retained by Google or other payment/subscription service providers according to their own policies and applicable legal requirements.

Deletion Processing

Account deletion is initiated from within the Technology World app. Data that can be immediately deleted is removed as part of the deletion process. Certain data stored by third-party providers may be subject to their processing and retention schedules.

If additional information is required to complete a deletion request, we may contact you using the contact information associated with your account.

Need Help?

If you cannot access your account or cannot use the in-app deletion option, contact Technology World and we will assist you with the account deletion process.
