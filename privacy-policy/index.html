Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Technology World collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application.

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Technology World ("we", "us", "our") provides educational content and learning resources related to programming and technology.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Technology World mobile application ("App"), how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

By using Technology World, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

If you create or use an account, we may collect information needed to create and maintain your account, such as your email address, display name, or authentication information.

Learning and App Usage Information

We may collect information about your interaction with the App, such as courses viewed, lessons completed, learning progress, preferences, and other information required to provide learning functionality.

Device and Technical Information

Depending on the features and services enabled in the App, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as device type, operating system version, app version, language, and technical diagnostic information.

Information You Provide

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, submit feedback, or otherwise communicate with us.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Technology World.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Save and synchronize learning progress.
  • Provide courses, lessons, and educational content.
  • Process and manage subscriptions.
  • Improve the performance, reliability, and functionality of the App.
  • Detect, prevent, and address security or technical issues.
  • Respond to support requests and user feedback.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

Technology World may offer optional paid subscriptions or other in-app purchases.

Payments are processed through the applicable platform's billing system, such as Google Play Billing. We do not directly receive or store your full payment card or bank account information through the App.

We may use third-party subscription management services, such as RevenueCat, to manage subscription status, entitlements, and purchase-related information.

Information handled by these services is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

5. Third-Party Services

Technology World may use third-party services to provide, maintain, secure, analyze, or improve certain parts of the App.

These services may include, where applicable:

  • Google Play services and Google Play Billing.
  • RevenueCat for subscription management.
  • Authentication or backend infrastructure providers.
  • Analytics, crash reporting, or performance monitoring services.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of services used by the App.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or allow access to information with service providers that help us operate the App, process subscriptions, provide infrastructure, maintain security, or perform other services on our behalf.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, our services, or others.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App and its services, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect our legitimate interests.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized in accordance with applicable requirements.

9. Your Choices and Data Deletion

Depending on the information and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information.

If you want to request deletion of your Technology World account or associated personal information, contact us using the details below.

Some information may need to be retained when required by law or for legitimate purposes such as fraud prevention, security, or financial record keeping.

Technology World is an educational application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

11. Notifications

If notification functionality is enabled, Technology World may use device notification services to send relevant app notifications. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our practices, or applicable legal requirements.
